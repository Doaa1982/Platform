# 1. Purpose

## 1.1 Objective

This document defines **how a Learning Workspace creates value**.

Unlike traditional software specifications that focus on features or modules, this document models the business lifecycle of an educational organisation.

A Value Stream represents a complete business outcome that delivers measurable value to a Learning Workspace.

---

## 1.2 Guiding Principle

The platform is **not** a Course Management System.

It is an **Educational Business Operating System (EduOS).**

Every capability should help educators build, operate, improve and grow an educational business.

# 4. Value Stream 1 — Launch Learning Business

## Business Goal

Enable an educator or organisation to establish an operational Learning Workspace.

---

## Trigger

A new customer joins the platform.

---

## Outcome

A fully configured Learning Workspace ready to serve learners.

---

## Primary Business Question

> "How do I start my learning business?"

---

## Capabilities

- Workspace Identity
- Workspace Branding
- Workspace Configuration
- Workspace Policies
- AI Configuration
- Payment Setup
- Workspace Publishing

---

## Typical Activities

- Register account
- Create workspace
- Configure branding
- Configure educational preferences
- Invite first team members
- Connect payment provider
- Publish workspace

---

## Success Metrics

- Time to first published workspace
- Workspace activation rate
- Workspace completion rate
- First learning product created

# 10. Workspace Maturity Model

The platform should evolve alongside the Learning Workspace.

Capabilities are introduced progressively as the organisation grows.

---

## Level 1 — Solo Educator

### Typical Profile

- One educator
- One brand
- Small learner base

### Primary Goals

- Launch quickly
- Teach efficiently
- Build reputation

### Core Capabilities

- Workspace
- Learning Products
- Scheduling
- Learning Delivery
- Basic Payments

---

## Level 2 — Professional Tutor

### Typical Profile

- Growing learner base
- Multiple programmes
- Consistent revenue

### Additional Capabilities

- Branding
- Analytics
- AI Assistant
- Automation
- Certificates
- CRM

---

## Level 3 — Tutor Team

### Typical Profile

- Multiple educators
- Shared operations

### Additional Capabilities

- Team Management
- Permissions
- Shared Resources
- Internal Communication
- Performance Reporting

---

## Level 4 — Academy

### Typical Profile

- Large learner community
- Multiple departments

### Additional Capabilities

- Organisational Structure
- Finance
- Department Management
- Advanced Reporting
- Compliance

---

## Level 5 — Educational Organisation

### Typical Profile

- Enterprise-scale operations

### Additional Capabilities

- Governance
- Multi-site Management
- Enterprise Analytics
- Advanced AI
- External Integrations
- Strategic Planning

# 12. Design Principles

## Business Outcome First

Every feature should contribute to a measurable business outcome.

---

## Value Stream Ownership

Every capability belongs to one or more value streams.

No capability should exist without supporting a business outcome.

---

## Continuous Growth

The platform must support customers from launch through enterprise-scale growth.

---

## AI-Native

AI should improve every value stream rather than existing as an isolated feature.

---

## Workspace-Centric

Every value stream exists to strengthen the Learning Workspace as an independent educational business.

# Learning Workspace Capability Model (Refinement)

**Version:** 1.2

> **Revision Note (v1.2):** Renamed "Tutor Workspace Experience Capability" (Section 2.3) to **Workspace Owner Experience Capability**, and updated its reference in the Core Differentiating Capabilities list (Section 3), to align with the Learning Workspace Domain Language & Business Ontology's terminology rule: "Workspace Owner" is preferred over "Tutor" when referring to business ownership of a Workspace. "Tutor" remains the correct term for the pedagogical/teaching role a Member may hold, and is unaffected elsewhere in this document.

**Purpose**

Defines the strategic capabilities required to operate a multi-tenant Learning Workspace platform where each tutor or education business can create and operate their own branded learning environment.

---

# 1. Capability Model Principles

## Principle 1 — Every Tutor Is a Workspace Owner

The platform should enable each tutor to feel:

> "This is my own teaching platform."

Capabilities must support:

- personal branding;
- teaching identity;
- content ownership;
- learner relationships;
- business operations.

---

## Principle 2 — Content Is a Strategic Asset

The platform does not only deliver courses.

It helps educators build reusable educational assets.

---

## Principle 3 — AI Is Embedded Into Workflows

AI should enhance:

- creation;
- delivery;
- assessment;
- communication;
- business decisions.

---
